# DaBoyz Bot - Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DaBoyz Bot ("the Bot") handles information when you interact with or use the Bot on Discord.

We aim to collect and process only the information necessary for the Bot to provide its functionality.

## 2. Information the bot May Access

Because DaBoyz Bot operates through Discord, it may receive certain information made available to Discord applications and bots.

Depending on the server configuration, permissions, and commands being used, this may include:
- Discord User ID
- Discord username, display name, and avatar
- Server/Guild ID
- Server name
- Role information
- Voice channel information
- Voice state information
- Information associated with interactions or commands sent to the bot

Discord explains that bots can have access to certain baseline server information, including basic member profile information, roles, message metadata, voice-channel membership, and reactions, depending on the bot's configuration and access.

## 3. Information Collected Through Commands

When you interact with DaBoyz Bot, Discord may provide information necessary to process that interaction.

For example:
/version 
The /version command provides the currently reported DaBoyz Bot version.

The command does not require us to collect or permanently store personal information.

/joincall
The /joincall command may use your Discord user ID and current voice-channel information to determine which voice channel the Bot should join.

The Bot uses this information solely to perform the requested action.

DaBoyz Bot does not intentionally record, store, or distribute voice communications.

## 4. Information We Store

DaBoyz Bot is designed to minimize persistent storage of user information.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in a future update, we do **not intentionally maintain a database containing personal user profiles, private messages, or voice recordings for ordinary Bot operation.

Temporary technical information may exist in system logs or Discord's own systems as necessary to operate, troubleshoot, secure, or maintain the Bot.

## 5. How information is Used

Information available to the Bot may be used to:
- Execute commands
- Provide requested functionality
- Determine a user's server or voice channel context
- Maintain and troubleshoot the bot
- Detect abuse or anauthorized use
- Maintain the security and reliability of the bot
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use information collected through the Bot to sell personal information or create advertising profiles.

## 6. Voice Information

DaBoyz Bot may interact with Discord voice channels when a user requests that the Bot join their current voice channel.

The Bot's voice functionality does not intentionally record or store voice conversations.

Joining a voice channel does not by itself mean that the Bot is recording or storing the audio of people in that channel.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent information obtained through DaBoyz Bot.

Information may be processed by:
- Discord, as part of providing the Discord platform and API.
- Hosting or infrastructure providers necessary to operate the Bot.
- Service providers necessary to maintain or secure the Bot.
- Authorities when required by applicable law.

Discord maintains its own Privacy Policy describing how Discord processes information within its services.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Information that is no longer necessary may be deleted or otherwise disposed of.

Because Discord itself operates the underlying platform, Discord may retain information independently according to its own policies.

## 9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with DaBoyz Bot against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your Choice

You can stop the Bot from interacting with you by:
- Removing the bot from a server
- Not using the Bot's commands
- Leaving a server where the Bot is installed

If you have a privacy-related question or request, you can contact the developer using the contact information below.

## 11. Children's Privacy

DaBoyz Bot is operated through Discord and is subject to Discord's age requirements and policies.

We do not intentionally collect additional personal information from children through the Bot beyond information made available to the Bot through Discord for its normal functionality.

## 12. Third-Party Services

DaBoyz Bot depends on Discord and potentially other infrastructure or hosting services.

Those services may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to DaBoyz Bot and does not govern Discord's own collection or processing of information.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The Last Updated date will be changed whenever the policy is materially updated.
